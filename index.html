Welcome to QuackViz Beta

      
QuackViz analyzes local data in your browser with DuckDB-WASM, ECharts, and MapLibre. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

      
    
        
  • Load CSV, JSON, NDJSON/JSONL, or Parquet files, or import a direct URL.
    • 
        
  • Write SQL, use local starter queries, or optionally ask AI for structured proposals.
    • 
        
  • Save charts, dashboards, reports, and portable local packages.
    • 
        
  • Local files may need to be re-imported after browser reloads.
